# Business Insights

## Insight 1

The source contains 4,803 movies; 3,229 (67.2%) have both positive budget and revenue and are eligible for the project's simplified profit/ROI calculations.

**Business implication:** Use this result as a screening signal, not a causal rule.

**Recommended action:** Validate with richer financial data including marketing, distribution and release-level data.

## Insight 2

Among financially eligible movies, 2,438 (75.5%) have revenue greater than recorded production budget. This is not equivalent to studio net profitability.

**Business implication:** Use this result as a screening signal, not a causal rule.

**Recommended action:** Validate with richer financial data including marketing, distribution and release-level data.

## Insight 3

Aggregate budget-based ROI across eligible movies is 198.2% (total profit divided by total eligible budget). This is a portfolio-weighted measure, not the average of individual ROI percentages.

**Business implication:** Use this result as a screening signal, not a causal rule.

**Recommended action:** Validate with richer financial data including marketing, distribution and release-level data.

## Insight 4

Median individual movie ROI is 130.0%. Individual ROI has extreme outliers, so median and aggregate ROI should be interpreted alongside distributions.

**Business implication:** Use this result as a screening signal, not a causal rule.

**Recommended action:** Validate with richer financial data including marketing, distribution and release-level data.

## Insight 5

Budget and revenue have a Pearson correlation of 0.71 among eligible movies. This is an association, not evidence that budget causes revenue.

**Business implication:** Use this result as a screening signal, not a causal rule.

**Recommended action:** Validate with richer financial data including marketing, distribution and release-level data.

## Insight 6

Audience rating and revenue have a Pearson correlation of 0.19 among eligible movies with votes. Rating is not a direct measure of commercial performance.

**Recommended action:** Validate with richer financial data including marketing, distribution and release-level data.

## Insight 7

TMDB popularity and revenue have a Pearson correlation of 0.60 among financially eligible movies. Popularity is a platform metric and should not be interpreted as causal.
